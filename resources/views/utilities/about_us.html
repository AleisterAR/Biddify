Buying and selling on Biddify

Biddify is an open saleroom for special, hard-to-find objects: any collector can list, and any collector can bid. Bids happen live, and we look after the payment and the delivery so both sides can trust the sale.

For buyers

  1. 1

    Bid while the lot is live

    Each lot has a closing time. Bids appear for everyone instantly, and you're notified if someone outbids you.

  2. 2

    Pay for what you won

    Pay by card through Stripe. Biddify holds your payment until 3 days after you've received the object, or until the seller confirms it was picked up and no complaint was raised.

  3. 3

    Track it to your door

    Add your delivery address and follow the parcel with its tracking number under Shipments.

For sellers

  1. 1

    List your object

    Add photos, a description and a provenance document to your inventory, then choose when bidding opens and closes.

  2. 2

    Ship once it's paid for

    When we confirm the buyer's payment, you'll get their address and a shipping label. Hand the parcel to the carrier and mark it as shipped.

  3. 3

    Get paid

    Once tracking confirms the buyer received the object, the payment is released to your account.
