Biddify is the most-visited curated marketplace in Europe for special objects, offering over 75,000 objects for auction each week. Our mission is to provide an exciting and seamless experience to our customers for buying and selling special, hard-to-find objects.

Safe, Secure Payments

We work with payment providers to handle all payments in a safe and secure manner.

For Buyers: Biddify holds your payment until 3 days after you have received the object or until the seller has confirmed the object was picked up and no complaint was received.

For Sellers: Once Biddify confirms we have received payment from the buyer, you may ship your object. Our system will then confirm the buyer has received their purchase - based on a valid Track & Trace - and the payment will be deposited into your account.
